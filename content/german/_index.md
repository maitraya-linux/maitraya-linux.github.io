### Lessons
- [YourGermanTeacher](https://www.youtube.com/@yourgermanteacher/playlists): An excellent YouTube channel with grammar and vocabulary lessons upto the high intermediate level (B2).
- [Learn German with Herr Antrim](https://www.youtube.com/@MrLAntrim/playlists): Another great Youtube channel with courses upto an early intermediate level (B1). Of special interest are the [crash courses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ka_3Rq8JZ4&list=PLUbX6-E9GbtopS8yWocmLgW6KPHrKBi_-).

### Audio lessons
- [Coffee Break German](https://feeds.acast.com/public/shows/0c3c53a1-180f-435a-9453-cec3883b4ada): A "hands free" resource ideal for learning vocabulary while commuting. Gently paced and therefore highly recommended.

### Grammar
- [Lingolia](https://deutsch.lingolia.com/en/): A comprehensive reference for every grammar topic conceivable. Also has vocabulary and comprehension sections.

### Shadowing
- [Slow German Podcast](https://slowgerman.com/podcast-hoeren/): A slow podcast with transcripts.

### Immersion
- Nicos Weg [A1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrcZ2ud4T3o&list=PLs7zUO7VPyJ6eoN6SmB1UcwvPUagK87ix) / [A2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrcZ2ud4T3o&list=PLs7zUO7VPyJ7YxTq_g2Rcl3Jthd5bpTdY) / [B1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrcZ2ud4T3o&list=PLs7zUO7VPyJ5razSfhOUVbTv9q6SAuPx-): An extremely well produced collection of immersion videos from A1 to B1. Additional resources and Q&A available on the [DW website](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/s-9095).
- [Easy German](https://www.youtube.com/@EasyGerman/videos): A YouTube channel with subtitled converations with native German speakers. Also explores typical everyday situations.
### Practice
- Begegnungen Online Excercises [A1+](https://www.schubert-verlag.de/aufgaben/uebungen_a1/a1_uebungen_index.htm) / [A2+](https://www.schubert-verlag.de/aufgaben/uebungen_a2/a2_uebungen_index.htm) / [B1+](https://www.schubert-verlag.de/aufgaben/uebungen_b1/sb1_uebungen_index.htm): A collection of online excercises complementing the book series.
- [Goethe Institute `Deutsch für dich`](https://www.goethe.de/ins/gb/en/spr/ueb.html): Interactive exercises for all language levels.
